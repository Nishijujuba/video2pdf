## Immutable rules

- `no_meta_writing_content`: Reader-facing content discusses the subject itself without exposing generation instructions, review strategy, rewriting rationale, or acceptance mechanics unless that process is the subject.
- `core_term_first_use_readability`: A core term first appears with enough natural-language context for the intended reader to understand its purpose, boundary, or use before compressed notation or unexplained shorthand carries the explanation.
- `delivery_glossary_term_strategy`: Every governed term follows its declared body display strategy and English-preservation location throughout the reader-facing artifact.
- `argument_chain_integrity`: Each material claim uses a predicate that fits its subject and semantic domain, preserves lifecycle stage and modifier dimensions, states modal strength supported by evidence, and exposes enough reasoning for the conclusion boundary to follow.
- `formula_information_gain`: Every reader-facing formula adds usable quantitative, algorithmic, constraint, threshold, dependency, comparison, or decision value that justifies its cognitive cost.
- `credibility_disclosure_content`: Required credibility disclosures state the material limitation accurately, concisely, and in reader-facing language without exposing irrelevant generation mechanics.

The projection grants semantic decision authority only when its kind is evaluation. Unknown findings are Contract Gaps.
